# Quantified Market Trends

These comparisons describe the eight selected items. They do not establish effects of anti-cheat enforcement or represent the full Grand Exchange.

## Main findings

Mean daily observed volume was lower than the early-window mean for 6 of 8 items in the first half of 2026 and 7 in July. The July exception was Ruby necklace. This supports a broad decline in the selected sample rather than a decline for every item.

July mean midpoint prices increased for 3 of 4 members-only items. Among F2P-accessible items, 3 declined and 1 increased. The item-level results are more varied than a simple division between rising members prices and stagnant F2P prices.

These means compare levels across windows. They do not by themselves establish a steady decline within the first half of 2026, reduced volatility, or statistical significance.

Each panel uses the first 28 observations as its reference of 100. Daily observations remain visible behind a trailing seven-day mean. The first six smoothed values are omitted because a full week is not available. Shared vertical scales support comparison within each figure. Original charts retain their original normalization and remain available.

A price index above 100 is above its selected reference period, not above a market-wide average. The midpoint is an unweighted mean of the two reported transaction-price averages. F2P accessibility describes items rather than the account types of traders. Trends and associations alone cannot isolate enforcement from updates, seasonality, player activity, demand, or changes in data coverage.
